About Us

More Than Karaoke — A Complete Event Hall

📍 Just 2 Minutes' Walk from Kurla Station

9 Star Karaoke & Multipurpose Auditorium is not just a karaoke studio — it's Kurla's go-to multipurpose event hall. Our full air-conditioned hall comfortably seats 100 guests and is fully equipped for everything from a quiet corporate meeting to a full-blown wedding celebration.

We regularly host live karaoke & live singing shows with live music instruments, open-mic nights, corporate events, birthday parties, haldi ceremonies, kitty parties, get-togethers, and small wedding functions — all with a professional sound system, separate washrooms for men & women, and catering on request. We also run Bollywood and classical singing & instrument classes for anyone who wants to learn.

  • 🎵 Live karaoke & live singing shows
  • 👥 Full A/C hall, seating for 100 guests
  • 🏆 Corporate events, seminars & meetings
  • 💎 Weddings, birthdays, haldi & kitty parties
  • 🎧 Bollywood & classical singing classes
